question: Can I sell part of my land?
category: selling
order: 13
active: true
---

Usually yes, but two things decide it: your county's minimum lot size, and whether the piece you are splitting off has its own legal road access. Splitting a parcel requires a survey and, in most Tennessee counties, subdivision approval before the new lot can be sold separately.

If the back half is landlocked, it is far harder to sell and worth much less — buyers cannot finance or build on ground they cannot legally reach. An easement can solve it, but it has to be recorded, not a handshake with a neighbor.

Subdividing is most of what we do, so tell us what you are working with.
