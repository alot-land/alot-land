Land value comes down to four things: what comparable parcels nearby actually sold for, whether you have legal road access, what the county will let you build, and how far utilities are. Zillow estimates are unreliable on raw land — they are built for houses with bedroom counts, not acreage.

The fastest honest answer is recent sales of similar-sized parcels in your county, adjusted for access and terrain. Two adjoining tracts can differ by 40% in value if one has road frontage and the other is landlocked. Wooded, steep, or wet ground appraises well below open, buildable ground.
