question: How do I sell land without a realtor?
category: selling
order: 12
active: true
---

You can sell land yourself, and on smaller parcels it often makes more sense than listing it. Commissions on land run 6–10%, higher than houses, because land takes longer to move. Selling direct means you agree a price with a buyer and a title company handles the deed, title search, and closing.

The title company is the part people skip and regret. They confirm you are the legal owner, clear any liens or back taxes, and record the deed properly. That is what makes the sale stick.

Selling directly to us removes the listing step entirely — no photos, no showings, no waiting on a buyer's financing.
